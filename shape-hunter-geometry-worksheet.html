Shape Hunter Geometry Worksheet

A printable primary geometry activity that helps students identify, draw, count, and describe common 2D shapes after playing Shape Hunter.

2D shapes • sides and corners • classroom shape hunt

About This Worksheet

The Shape Hunter Geometry Worksheet gives students a simple way to connect digital gameplay with pencil-and-paper thinking. After students practise identifying shapes in the Shape Hunter game, they can use this worksheet to draw shapes, count sides and corners, find real-life examples, and explain their favourite shape.

This resource works well for math centres, small-group review, whole-class follow-up, early-finisher work, or at-home practice.

What Students Practise

  • Recognizing common 2D shapes such as circles, squares, triangles, and rectangles.
  • Drawing shapes with care and attention to their properties.
  • Counting sides and corners to compare different shapes.
  • Finding real-life objects that match classroom geometry vocabulary.
  • Explaining shape choices using simple written reflection.

Suggested Lesson Flow

  1. Warm up: Review the names of common 2D shapes with the class.
  2. Play: Let students play Shape Hunter and identify shapes on screen.
  3. Discuss: Ask students which shapes were easiest or hardest to find.
  4. Complete: Students finish the worksheet independently or with a partner.
  5. Share: Invite students to share one real-life shape they found in the room.

Differentiation Ideas

Extra Support

Provide shape cards, traceable examples, or a word bank with shape names. Students can also work with a partner during the classroom shape hunt.

Extra Challenge

Ask students to add more shapes such as pentagons, hexagons, ovals, or trapezoids. They can also describe how two shapes are the same and different.

Assessment Ideas

Teachers can use the completed worksheet as a quick check for shape recognition, geometry vocabulary, and understanding of sides and corners. The reflection question also shows whether students can explain their thinking in a simple sentence.
