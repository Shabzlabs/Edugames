How Teachers Can Use ShabzLabs

ShabzLabs games can be used during math centres, small-group practice, whole-class lessons, early-finisher activities, review periods, or at-home practice. Each game focuses on a clear learning skill, and the teacher resources help students record their thinking, practise the concept on paper, and reflect on what they learned.

  • Before playing: introduce the skill, model one example, and explain the goal of the game.
  • During playing: students practise independently, with a partner, or on a classroom smart board.
  • After playing: students complete a worksheet, answer reflection questions, or explain their strategy.

Simple Lesson Flow

Teachers can turn any ShabzLabs game into a quick classroom activity using this simple structure:

  • Mini lesson: introduce the concept and vocabulary for 5–10 minutes.
  • Game practice: students play the related ShabzLabs game individually, with a partner, or as a class.
  • Worksheet or recording task: students write, draw, tally, graph, or explain their thinking.
  • Share and reflect: students discuss strategies, mistakes, and what they noticed.

Differentiation Ideas

ShabzLabs activities can be adjusted for different learners by changing the number of questions, allowing partner work, using manipulatives, reading instructions aloud, or giving students extra time to explain their thinking with pictures and words.

  • For extra support: use fewer questions, visuals, manipulatives, and teacher-guided examples.
  • For independent practice: students complete the game and worksheet on their own.
  • For extension: students create their own questions, explain a strategy, or design a new challenge for a classmate.

Assessment Ideas

Teachers can use ShabzLabs resources for quick formative assessment. Student worksheets, exit tickets, drawings, tally charts, graphs, and written explanations can show what students understand and where they may need more practice.

Teacher FAQ

Are the resources free?

Yes. ShabzLabs teacher resources are free for classroom and learning use.

Do students need an account?

No. Students can play ShabzLabs games without creating an account, signing in, or entering personal information.

Can I use the games on a smart board?

Yes. The games can be used on a classroom display for whole-class lessons, small groups, centres, review, or independent practice.

How should I use the worksheets?

Use the worksheets before, during, or after gameplay. They help students record answers, explain strategies, and connect the game to classroom learning.

Teacher Tip

Try pairing one game with one short follow-up task. For example, after students play Spinner Adventure, they can spin 10 times, record tallies, colour a graph, and compare which result happened most and least often.
